# SEO Audit Framework

The SEO audit framework is used to evaluate a fictional client's website before creating an organic growth plan.

Audit sections:

- Crawlability and indexation.
- Site architecture.
- Metadata quality.
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals.
- Internal linking.
- Content coverage.
- Duplicate content.
- Structured data.
- Backlink risk review.
- Local SEO signals where relevant.

Findings must be classified by impact and effort:

- High impact, low effort.
- High impact, high effort.
- Medium impact.
- Low impact.

The final audit summary should include no more than ten prioritized recommendations. Technical details can be placed in an appendix.
