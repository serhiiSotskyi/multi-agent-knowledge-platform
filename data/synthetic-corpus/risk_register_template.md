# Risk Register Template

Every project should maintain a simple risk register. Risks should be written in plain language and assigned an owner.

Fields:

- Risk ID.
- Description.
- Area: PPC, SEO, analytics, content, client dependency, technical dependency.
- Impact: low, medium, high.
- Likelihood: low, medium, high.
- Owner.
- Mitigation.
- Status.

Example risks:

- Conversion tracking is not verified before PPC launch.
- CMS access is delayed and technical SEO fixes cannot be applied.
- Client has not approved ad copy.
- Landing pages are too slow for mobile traffic.
- Product feed contains missing prices or incorrect availability.
