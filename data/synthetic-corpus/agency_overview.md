# Northstar Digital Growth: Agency Overview

Northstar Digital Growth is a fictional PPC and SEO agency used as a synthetic enterprise corpus for ModelWeave demonstrations.

The agency provides paid search management, paid social campaign support, SEO audits, technical SEO consulting, content planning, landing page recommendations, analytics reporting, and monthly growth reviews.

The company serves small and medium-sized commercial clients in sectors such as local services, ecommerce, SaaS, education, and professional services. All client names and processes in this corpus are fictional.

Core teams:

- PPC Strategy
- SEO Strategy
- Analytics
- Client Success
- Content Planning
- Technical Operations

The agency's internal knowledge base contains service definitions, onboarding checklists, campaign setup notes, reporting templates, quality standards, and sample client scenarios.
