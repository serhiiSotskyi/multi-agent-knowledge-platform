# Monthly Reporting Policy

Monthly reports are written for business readers, not only marketing specialists. Each report must explain what changed, why it changed, and what action is recommended next.

Required PPC metrics:

- Spend.
- Impressions.
- Clicks.
- Cost per click.
- Conversions.
- Cost per conversion.
- Conversion rate.
- Search term highlights.

Required SEO metrics:

- Organic sessions.
- Organic conversions.
- Ranking movement for tracked topics.
- Indexed page changes.
- Technical issue status.
- Content published or refreshed.

Each report must include:

- Executive summary.
- Performance table.
- Key wins.
- Key risks.
- Next-month priorities.

Reports should avoid unsupported claims. If the cause of a change is uncertain, the report must state that it is a hypothesis.
