# Client Onboarding Checklist

The onboarding process begins after a fictional client signs a service agreement. The goal is to collect enough context to prepare PPC and SEO work without relying on informal messages.

Required onboarding inputs:

- Business description and target locations.
- Primary products or services.
- Monthly advertising budget range.
- Main competitors known to the client.
- Website URL and CMS access level.
- Analytics access status.
- Existing Google Ads or Meta Ads account access.
- SEO history and known technical issues.
- Brand guidelines and prohibited claims.
- Primary conversion actions.

The Client Success Manager creates a project record, assigns a PPC lead if paid media is included, assigns an SEO lead if organic search work is included, and schedules an internal kickoff.

Acceptance criteria:

- All required account access is either granted or explicitly marked as pending.
- Initial risks are logged before strategy work begins.
- The first reporting date is defined.
- The client has received a summary of next steps.
