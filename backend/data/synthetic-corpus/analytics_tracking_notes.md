# Analytics Tracking Notes

Conversion tracking is required before reliable PPC optimization. A campaign can be built before tracking is complete, but it should not be launched until the tracking status is confirmed.

Common conversion actions:

- Contact form submission.
- Phone call click.
- Demo request.
- Trial booking.
- Ecommerce purchase.
- Newsletter signup.

Tracking status categories:

- Verified.
- Installed but not tested.
- Missing.
- Blocked by access.
- Not applicable.

The analytics team records tracking status in the project notes and notifies the PPC lead if the launch date is affected.
