# Sample Client: Harbor Homeware

Harbor Homeware is a fictional ecommerce store selling kitchen accessories and home organization products.

Commercial goals:

- Increase ecommerce revenue from paid search.
- Improve organic traffic to category pages.
- Reduce shopping campaign waste.
- Prepare seasonal campaign plans.

Known constraints:

- Product feed quality is inconsistent.
- Some product pages have thin descriptions.
- The brand has limited historical SEO data.
- Seasonal demand changes strongly by month.

Recommended first workflow:

1. Audit product feed attributes.
2. Review category-page metadata.
3. Segment shopping campaigns by product margin.
4. Build negative keyword rules.
5. Create content briefs for top category pages.
