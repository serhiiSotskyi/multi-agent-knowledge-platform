# SEO Content Brief Template

A content brief defines the purpose and structure of an SEO page before writing begins.

Required sections:

- Target topic.
- Search intent.
- Primary audience.
- Main keyword cluster.
- Secondary keyword cluster.
- Suggested title.
- Page objective.
- Outline.
- Internal link targets.
- Evidence or examples required.
- Conversion call to action.

Quality rules:

- The brief must not require unsupported claims.
- The outline must match search intent.
- The recommended call to action must be relevant to the page topic.
- Internal links should point to existing relevant pages.
