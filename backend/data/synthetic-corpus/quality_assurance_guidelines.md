# Quality Assurance Guidelines

All client-facing outputs must be reviewed before delivery.

General QA:

- The document answers the original request.
- Recommendations are specific and actionable.
- Unsupported claims are removed or marked as hypotheses.
- Terminology is consistent.
- Confidential or irrelevant information is not included.

PPC QA:

- Budget references match approved budget.
- Conversion tracking status is clear.
- Risks are stated before launch recommendations.

SEO QA:

- Recommendations are prioritized.
- Technical issues include expected business impact.
- Content suggestions match search intent.

RAG answer QA:

- Answers should cite retrieved source documents.
- If no relevant source is found, the assistant should say so.
- The assistant should not invent client facts.
