Audience: diploma small-defense committee and supervisor.

Objective: explain the approved thesis topic, show why the architecture matters, summarize methods and implementation, prove results with deployment/test evidence, and end with a safe demo link.

Narrative arc: commercial LLM systems require controlled architecture, not a chatbot; ModelWeave demonstrates this through a service-oriented PPC/SEO AI workforce with RAG, multi-agent workflows, human approval gates, testing evidence, and production deployment.

Slide list:
1. Topic
2. Goal and tasks
3. Relevance
4. Methods and comparison
5. Structural scheme of the software algorithm
6. Results
7. Conclusions
8. Demo link

Source plan: use thesis title and structure from `ModelWeave_Diploma_Thesis_EN_Full.docx`, visual evidence from `evidence/figures`, screenshots from `evidence/screenshots`, metrics from `evidence/tests`, and production links from project memory.

Visual system: white academic/product deck with dark navy text, restrained teal/blue accents, thin gray dividers, compact evidence cards, and screenshot/diagram proof objects. No real company data is shown.

Editability plan: slide titles, labels, metrics, bullet text, comparison content, demo steps, and speaker notes are editable PowerPoint objects; screenshots, QR code, and diagrams are embedded images.
